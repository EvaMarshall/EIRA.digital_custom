Types of Builds

Custom

Clean, sustainable websites built with intention. From simple branded single page sites to fully custom builds that reflect your work and are designed to last. Custom sites are a great choice when you want something fast, lightweight and free from the limits of templates or plugins. I focus on creating clear, calm layouts that are easy to navigate and simple to maintain, giving you a site that feels consistent, reliable and genuinely yours.

Read build example

EIRA.digital was built using Astro, HTML, Tailwind and minimal JavaScript for the responsive elements. It is hosted on my Infomaniak platform, a provider that prioritises security and is powered by 100% renewable energy.

The beauty of a fully custom build for a simple showcase site like this is that it massively reduces maintenance compared to CMSs like WordPress, while giving you complete freedom in design. No themes. No lock‑in. No bloat.

Custom builds also load faster, stay more secure and use fewer server resources. This means a lighter environmental footprint and a smoother experience for your visitors. Once designed and built, the site remains fast, stable and reliable, looking great for years with minimal upkeep.

Shopify

Fast, mobile first Shopify stores that are easy to run, simple to update and built to support your business as it grows. Shopify is a great choice for small brands because it handles the complicated parts for you, like hosting, security and checkout, while giving you a clean place to manage your products and orders. My approach focuses on keeping everything organised, lightweight and easy to maintain so your store stays fast and reliable without extra work.

Read build example

Prestacycle.eu was built as part of a full redesign and expansion of the Prestacycle US store. I created a new visual system, refreshed the layout and improved the overall structure of the theme. Once the new design was complete, I redeployed it across the EU, UK and US stores to bring consistency, clarity and a more modern feel to the brand.

The build uses metaobjects, metafields and custom code to make the theme more sophisticated and far easier to manage. Product data is structured cleanly, content is reusable and updates can be made quickly without touching the theme files. This keeps the store flexible as the catalogue grows and ensures the team can manage everything with confidence.

Shopify is an excellent choice for online stores and multi‑region brands like Prestacycle. It provides secure hosting, reliable checkout, fast global delivery of assets and a stable foundation that scales without extra maintenance. Once set up, the store remains fast, dependable and easy to run, giving the business more time to focus on products and customers rather than technical upkeep.

WordPress

Clean, modern WordPress builds that feel clearer, load smoothly and are easier to look after. WordPress works well when you want the freedom to update your own content without touching code, and it gives you a familiar editing experience that grows with your site. My approach focuses on simplifying themes, reducing unnecessary plugins and creating a layout that feels calm and consistent, so your site stays reliable and easy to manage over time.

Read build example

Ostaratherapy.co.uk was a full redesign. Hannah wanted a clearer layout, a more aesthetic design and a sense of consistency across every page. I rebuilt the site with a calmer visual language, improved spacing and typography, and a structure that feels more intuitive for visitors. The aim was to create a space that reflects her practice and feels welcoming from the first click.

The build focused on simplifying the theme, reducing unnecessary plugins and improving the overall flow of the site. Content was reorganised so it is easier for Hannah to update, and the design now feels cohesive across her pages. I also set up flexible user access, so she can update as much or as little as she wants without worrying about breaking anything. Everything is designed to be manageable without technical knowledge.

Behind the scenes, everything runs on Infomaniak’s renewable-powered hosting, giving you a secure and environmentally responsible foundation. The build is kept lightweight and structured, so the site stays fast and dependable as it grows. You can update content yourself or hand things over to me — whatever feels right for your workflow.
